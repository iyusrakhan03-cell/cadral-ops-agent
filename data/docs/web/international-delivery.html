International Delivery

Last updated: 2 April 2026

We're a UK company based in Leeds, but we're happy to send our homeware to a small number of European countries. Here's everything you need to know before ordering from outside the UK.

Where we deliver

We currently deliver to:

  • Republic of Ireland
  • France
  • Germany
  • Netherlands
  • Belgium

We're unable to deliver to any other countries at the moment, including the USA, Canada, Australia, Spain and Italy. We also can't deliver to BFPO addresses.

Delivery times are counted from dispatch. Orders placed before 1pm on a UK working day are dispatched the same day, and we'll email you a tracking link once your parcel is on its way.

Customs, duties and VAT

For orders to the EU, import VAT and any customs duties are calculated and collected at checkout. This is known as Delivered Duty Paid (DDP). It means the total you see at checkout is the total you pay – there are no extra charges or customs fees to pay when your parcel arrives.

Prices are shown and charged in pounds sterling (GBP). Your card provider may apply its own exchange rate or foreign transaction fee.

Items we can't send abroad

Large furniture and storage items marked "2-person delivery" can't be shipped internationally. If your basket contains one of these items, you'll be asked to remove it before you can check out with an international address.

International returns

You can return most items within 30 days of delivery, unused and in their original packaging, just like our UK customers. However, our free prepaid return labels are only available in the UK, so international customers pay their own return postage.

If your item is faulty or arrived damaged, we'll cover the cost of returning it. Please contact us first so we can arrange this – damaged items must be reported within 48 hours of delivery, with photos of the item and packaging.

We recommend using a tracked service when sending a return, as we can't refund items that don't reach us.

Christmas ordering

The last order date for international delivery before Christmas 2026 is 10 December 2026.
