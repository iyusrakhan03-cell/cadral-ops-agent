Our Approach to Sustainability

Last updated: 20 March 2026

Since we started Thistle & Pine in Leeds in 2019, our aim has been simple: to sell homeware that's made to last, and to send it out in a way that's kinder to the planet. We haven't got everything right yet, but here's what we're doing today.

Plastic-free packaging

Since 2023, all of our packaging has been 100% plastic-free. Orders are packed in recycled cardboard boxes, with paper-based void fill and paper tape instead of bubble wrap and plastic tape.

Everything that arrives with your order can go straight into your household paper and cardboard recycling. We'd also love you to reuse our boxes – they're sturdy enough for storage or for sending a return.

Carbon-neutral delivery

Every delivery we send is carbon neutral. We measure the emissions from getting your parcel to your door and offset them through our offsetting partner, which supports verified carbon-reduction projects. This applies to Standard, Express, 2-person and international deliveries alike.

Offsetting isn't a complete answer, so we also try to reduce emissions in the first place – for example, by dispatching orders as one parcel wherever possible.

Better materials

European flax linen

Our linen bedding and bath linen are made from European flax, a crop that needs relatively little water to grow. Our linen is OEKO-TEX certified, which means it has been tested for harmful substances.

Made to last

The most sustainable product is the one you don't need to replace. That's why our Pine Pro cookware comes with a 10-year guarantee against manufacturing defects, and why we publish detailed care guides for cast iron, wooden boards, candles and bedding to help everything you buy from us last longer.

Old-pan recycling scheme

Old pans are difficult to recycle at home, so we'll take them off your hands. When you buy from our Pine Pro cookware range, you can send us an old pan from any brand and we'll make sure it's recycled responsibly.

  1. Place your Pine Pro order.
  2. Request an old-pan return label from our Customer Care team, quoting your Pine Pro order number.
  3. Pack your old pan in a box (one of ours is ideal) and attach the label.
  4. Once it reaches us, we'll send you a code for 10% off.

The pan doesn't need to be from Thistle & Pine, and it doesn't need to be in working order.

Looking ahead

We'll continue to review our suppliers, materials and delivery partners, and we'll update this page as we make progress. If you have ideas or questions about our sustainability work, please email support@thistleandpine.co.uk – we read every message.
