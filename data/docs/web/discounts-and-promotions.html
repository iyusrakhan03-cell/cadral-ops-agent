Discounts & Promotions

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Here's a round-up of the ways you can save at Thistle & Pine, along with the small print that applies to all our offers.

Black Friday 2026

Our Black Friday sale runs from Friday 27 November to Tuesday 1 December 2026. During the sale you'll get 20% off sitewide.

  • The discount is shown on product pages and taken off automatically in your basket – no code needed.
  • The offer excludes gift cards and our Pine Pro cookware range.
  • Because only one discount can be used per order, other discount codes (such as student or newsletter codes) can't be added on top of Black Friday prices.
  • Our normal delivery charges apply, and Standard UK delivery is still free on orders of £50 or more.

Black Friday purchases are covered by our usual 30-day returns policy. Orders placed from 1 November 2026 also benefit from our Christmas extended returns, so you can return them until 31 January 2027.

Newsletter sign-up: 10% off your first order

Sign up to our newsletter and we'll email you a code for 10% off your first order. You'll also hear about new collections, care tips and seasonal offers first. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link at the bottom of every email.

Student discount: 10% off

Students get 10% off with a verified student email address ending in .ac.uk. Verify your email through the Student Discount page and we'll send your code.

NHS & Blue Light discount: 15% off

To thank NHS staff and the emergency services, we offer 15% off. You'll need to verify your eligibility first through our NHS & Blue Light page, after which a code will be sent to you.

Business and bulk orders

Ordering 20 or more units of the same item, or spending over £1,000? Email business@thistleandpine.co.uk for our tiered business discount: 5% off orders over £1,000 and 10% off orders over £5,000.

Discount code rules

  • Only one discount code can be used per order.
  • Codes must be entered at checkout. They can't be applied after an order has been placed, and we can't refund the difference if you forgot to use one.
  • Discount codes can't be exchanged for cash.
  • Codes can't be used to buy gift cards.

Price matching

We don't price match with other retailers. We work hard to keep our prices fair all year round.

My code isn't working

Check that the code has been typed exactly as it appears, with no spaces, and that the items in your basket aren't excluded from the offer. If you're still having trouble, contact us before placing your order and we'll take a look.
