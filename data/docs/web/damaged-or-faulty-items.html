Damaged or Faulty Items

Last updated: 9 June 2026

We pack every order carefully, but occasionally something arrives damaged or develops a fault. We're sorry if that's happened to you – here's how we'll put it right.

Items damaged in transit

If your item arrives broken, cracked, dented or otherwise damaged, please let us know within 48 hours of delivery. To help us resolve it quickly, send us:

  • your order number
  • a photo of the damaged item
  • a photo of the packaging, including the outer box and shipping label

You can send these by email to support@thistleandpine.co.uk or upload them through live chat on our website. Please keep the item and packaging until we've been in touch, as the courier may need to see them.

Faulty items

A faulty item is one that isn't of satisfactory quality, isn't fit for purpose or doesn't match its description. Your rights are protected by the Consumer Rights Act 2015:

When the fault appearsWhat you're entitled to
Within 30 days of deliveryA full refund
From 30 days up to 6 monthsA repair or replacement (or a refund if that isn't possible)
After 6 monthsCovered by our product warranty

All our products come with a 12-month warranty from delivery. Furniture and electrical items such as kettles, toasters and lamps have a 2-year warranty, and our Pine Pro cookware carries a 10-year guarantee against manufacturing defects.

Returning a damaged or faulty item

We pay the return postage for any damaged or faulty item. Once you've reported the problem, our team will send you a prepaid label or, for large 2-person delivery items, arrange a free collection – the usual £25 collection fee does not apply to faulty goods.

For low-value items costing under £20, we may refund you without needing the item back. We'll let you know if this applies to you.

If the item was faulty, we'll also refund the original delivery charge.

What isn't covered

Normal wear and tear, accidental damage and damage caused by not following the care instructions aren't treated as faults. For example:

  • rust or a damaged surface on cast iron that has been put in the dishwasher
  • warping or discolouration from overheating a pan on a high flame when empty
  • splitting of a wooden board that has been soaked in water
  • scratches on non-stick coatings caused by metal utensils

Our care guides explain how to look after each product so it lasts.

Safety concerns

If you believe a product has caused an injury, or poses a fire or safety hazard, stop using it immediately and contact us by phone on 0113 496 0321 during opening hours. A member of our team will handle your case personally.

How to report a problem

  1. Take clear photos of the item and the packaging.
  2. Contact us by email or live chat with your order number and photos.
  3. We'll confirm whether we need the item back and send a return label if so.
  4. Once approved, we'll refund or send a replacement.
