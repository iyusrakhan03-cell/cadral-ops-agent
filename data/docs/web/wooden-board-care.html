How to Care for Your Wooden Chopping Board

Last updated: 11 February 2026

A good wooden chopping board will last for years if it's looked after properly. Wood is a natural material that absorbs and releases moisture, so a little regular care keeps it smooth, hygienic and free from cracks. This guide applies to all our wooden chopping, serving and bread boards.

The golden rules

  • Hand wash only. Never put a wooden board in the dishwasher.
  • Never soak it. Don't leave your board in a sink of water or submerge it.
  • Oil it monthly with food-safe mineral oil.

Everyday cleaning

  1. Scrape off any food with the back of a knife or a scraper.
  2. Wash both sides with warm water, a little washing-up liquid and a sponge or brush.
  3. Rinse quickly under the tap.
  4. Dry straight away with a clean tea towel.
  5. Leave the board standing upright on its edge to air-dry fully, so both sides dry evenly.

Washing both sides, even if you only chopped on one, helps stop the board from warping.

After raw meat or fish

Wash thoroughly with hot soapy water straight after use. Many cooks prefer to keep one board just for raw meat and another for bread, fruit and vegetables.

Oiling your board

Oiling stops the wood from drying out and helps it resist moisture and stains. We recommend oiling your board once a month, or whenever it starts to look pale and dry.

  1. Make sure the board is clean and completely dry.
  2. Pour a small amount of food-safe mineral oil onto the surface.
  3. Rub it into the wood with a soft cloth, following the direction of the grain. Don't forget the edges and the underside.
  4. Leave it to soak in for a few hours, or overnight.
  5. Wipe away any excess oil with a clean cloth.

Avoid using cooking oils such as olive or sunflower oil, as they can turn rancid over time and leave an unpleasant smell.

Removing stains and smells

Sprinkle coarse salt over the board and scrub it with half a lemon. Rinse, dry thoroughly, and re-oil once dry.

What to avoid

  • Dishwashers – the heat and water will cause the wood to warp and split.
  • Soaking or leaving the board wet on the draining board.
  • Drying it next to a radiator, oven or in direct sunlight.
  • Using bleach or harsh chemical cleaners.

Warranty

Our wooden boards are covered by our standard 12-month warranty against manufacturing defects. Splitting or warping caused by dishwasher use or soaking isn't a manufacturing defect and isn't covered, so following this guide really does pay off.
