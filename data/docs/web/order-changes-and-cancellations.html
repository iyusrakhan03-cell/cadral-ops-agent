Changing or Cancelling an Order

Last updated: 22 July 2026

Changed your mind, or spotted a typo in your address? Here's what you can change, and when.

Before your order is dispatched

Until your order has left our warehouse, you can cancel it or change the delivery address free of charge. There are two ways to do this:

  1. Sign in to your account, go to My orders, select the order and choose Cancel order or Change address.
  2. Contact our Customer Care team by live chat, email (support@thistleandpine.co.uk) or phone (0113 496 0321) with your order number.

Please get in touch as quickly as possible. Orders placed before 1pm on a working day are dispatched the same day, so the window to make changes can be short.

If you cancel before dispatch, we refund the full amount, including any delivery charge, to your original payment method.

After your order is dispatched

Once your order has been dispatched it can no longer be cancelled, and we can't change the delivery address. You'll know your order has been dispatched because we'll have emailed you a tracking link. You still have options:

  • Refuse the delivery. If you no longer want the parcel, you can refuse it when the courier arrives. It will be returned to us and we'll refund you once it arrives back at our warehouse.
  • Return it. Accept the parcel and return it within 30 days of delivery using our free Returns Portal at thistleandpine.co.uk/returns.

Adding or swapping items

We can't add items to an order that has already been placed, or swap one item for another. If you've forgotten something, simply place a second order. If you'd like a different item instead, cancel the original (if it hasn't been dispatched) or return it, and place a new order.

Discount codes also can't be applied to an order after it has been placed, so please make sure your code is entered at checkout.

Your 14-day cancellation right

Under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013, you have the right to cancel an online purchase within 14 days without giving a reason. This period starts the day after you receive your goods. If you return your whole order within 14 days, we'll also refund the original delivery charge.

Our own 30-day returns policy goes further than the law requires, so most customers simply use the Returns Portal.

2-person delivery items

Large furniture sent by 2-person delivery can also be cancelled free of charge before dispatch. After dispatch, if you no longer want the item, we'll arrange collection. A £25 collection fee is deducted from your refund unless the item is faulty.
