Extended Christmas returns

Buying gifts early? Any order placed between 1 November and 24 December 2026 can be returned until 31 January 2027. Items must be unused and in their original packaging, and our usual non-returnable exclusions still apply (personalised or engraved items, opened pillows and duvets, gift cards and Final Sale items).

UK returns are free using a prepaid label from our Returns Portal at thistleandpine.co.uk/returns.
