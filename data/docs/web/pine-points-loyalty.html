Pine Points Loyalty Scheme

Last updated: 18 May 2026

Pine Points is our way of saying thank you. Every time you shop with us you earn points that you can turn into money off future orders.

Joining

Pine Points is free to join. All you need is a Thistle & Pine account – create one at checkout or from the My account page and you'll be enrolled automatically. Guest checkouts don't earn points, so make sure you're signed in before you order.

Earning points

  • Earn 1 point for every £1 you spend. Delivery charges don't count towards your points.
  • Receive a birthday bonus of 50 points. Add your date of birth in your account to make sure you get it.

Points are calculated on the amount you actually pay for your products, after any discounts.

Spending points

Every 100 points is worth £5 off. You can redeem points in multiples of 100 at checkout – for example 200 points for £10 off, or 300 points for £15 off.

To redeem, sign in, go to checkout and choose how many points you'd like to use. Your balance is always shown in the Pine Points section of your account.

Expiry

Your points don't expire as long as your account is active. If there's no activity on your account for 12 months, your points will expire. Placing an order or redeeming points counts as activity.

Returns and refunds

If you return an item and receive a refund, the points you earned on that item are deducted from your balance. If you used points to pay for part of an order, please contact us when returning it and we'll look at the best way to reinstate them.

Frequently asked questions

Can I earn points on gift cards?

Points are earned on what you spend, excluding delivery. If you have a question about a particular purchase, our Customer Care team can check your balance for you.

Can I transfer my points to someone else?

No. Pine Points are linked to your account and can't be transferred or exchanged for cash.

I forgot to sign in before ordering. Can I still get my points?

Contact us within 30 days of your order with your order number, and we'll check whether we can add the points to your account.
