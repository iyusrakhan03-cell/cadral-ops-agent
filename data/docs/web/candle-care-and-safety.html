Candle Care & Safety

Last updated: 29 September 2026

A little care goes a long way with candles. Follow these simple steps and you'll get a cleaner, more even burn, a stronger scent and the full burn time from every Thistle & Pine candle.

The first burn matters most

The first time you light your candle, let it burn for 2–3 hours, until the melted wax reaches the edges of the container. This is called a full melt pool.

Wax has a "memory". If you blow it out too early on the first burn, it will tend to melt only to that point every time afterwards, creating a tunnel down the middle and wasting wax around the sides.

Trim the wick

Before every burn, trim the wick to 5mm. A wick that's too long produces a large, flickering flame, soot and black marks on the glass. Use a wick trimmer or scissors, and remove the trimmings from the wax before lighting.

Don't burn for too long

Never burn a candle for more than 4 hours at a time. After 4 hours, carbon can build up on the wick, making the flame unstable and the container very hot. Blow it out, let it cool completely, trim the wick and relight later.

Candle safety

  • Never leave a burning candle unattended.
  • Keep candles out of reach of children and pets.
  • Place on a stable, heat-resistant surface, away from draughts, curtains, shelves and anything flammable.
  • Keep burning candles at least 10cm apart.
  • Don't move a candle while it's lit or while the wax is liquid.
  • Stop using the candle when around 1cm of wax remains at the bottom, as the container can overheat.
  • Extinguish with a snuffer or by gently blowing it out – never use water.

Storing your candles

Keep candles in a cool, dark place with the lid on. Heat and direct sunlight can fade the colour and weaken the fragrance.

Reusing the container

Once your candle has finished, pour a little boiling water into the container to loosen the remaining wax, let it cool, then pop the wax out. The clean container makes a lovely pot for pens, make-up brushes or small plants.

Something not right?

If your candle tunnels despite following this guide, or the wick won't stay lit, contact us with your order number and a photo. Candles are covered by our standard 12-month warranty.
