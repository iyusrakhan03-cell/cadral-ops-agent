Payment Options

Last updated: 14 September 2026

We want paying for your order to be quick, simple and secure. All our prices are shown in pounds sterling (GBP) and include VAT at 20%, so the price you see in your basket is the price you pay, plus any delivery charge.

Ways to pay

We accept the following payment methods at checkout:

  • Visa (credit and debit)
  • Mastercard (credit and debit)
  • American Express
  • PayPal
  • Apple Pay
  • Google Pay
  • Klarna Pay in 3
  • Thistle & Pine gift cards

When is payment taken?

Payment is taken in full when you place your order. If you later cancel before dispatch, or return items, we refund the money to your original payment method.

Klarna Pay in 3

Klarna lets you split the cost of your order into three interest-free instalments. Pay in 3 is available on orders between £30 and £1,000. Simply choose Klarna at checkout and follow the steps; Klarna will carry out its own checks before approving your purchase.

Returning a Klarna order

If you return items from an order paid with Klarna, your refund goes back to Klarna, which then updates your payment plan. Any instalments you have already paid towards the returned items will be adjusted by Klarna.

Klarna is a separate credit provider, so questions about your instalment schedule are best answered directly through the Klarna app or website.

Paying with a gift card

You can pay for all or part of your order with a Thistle & Pine e-gift card. Enter the code in the gift card box at checkout and the balance will be deducted from your total. If your order costs more than your gift card balance, you can pay the remainder with any of our other payment methods.

If you return items paid for with a gift card, the refund is issued as a new gift card rather than to a bank account.

Keeping your payment safe

Our checkout is encrypted and card payments are handled by our secure payment provider. We do not store your full card number on our systems.

We will never ask for your card details by phone, email or live chat. If you receive a message claiming to be from Thistle & Pine that asks for your card number, security code or online banking details, please do not reply. Forward it to support@thistleandpine.co.uk so we can look into it.

Business customers

If you're buying for a business, we can provide VAT invoices. Approved business customers can also apply for 30-day payment terms. Email business@thistleandpine.co.uk to find out more.

Payment problems

My payment was declined

Please check that your billing address matches the address registered with your card issuer, and that your card hasn't expired. If it still doesn't work, try another payment method or contact your bank, as we're unable to see why a payment has been declined.

I've been charged but didn't receive an order confirmation

Check your junk or spam folder first. If there's still no email after an hour, contact our Customer Care team with the email address you used and we'll check whether the order went through.
