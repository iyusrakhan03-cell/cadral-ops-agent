Contact Us

Last updated: 6 January 2026

Our friendly Customer Care team is based at our warehouse and office in Leeds. Whether you have a question about a product, need help with an order, or want to tell us how we're doing, we'd love to hear from you.

Ways to get in touch

Live chat

The quickest way to reach us. Click the chat icon in the bottom corner of any page on our website. Live chat is staffed during our opening hours, and you can upload photos directly into the chat – handy if something has arrived damaged.

Email

Email support@thistleandpine.co.uk. Please include your order number in the subject line so we can find your details quickly.

Phone

Call us on 0113 496 0321. Calls are charged at your standard network rate for an 0113 number.

Other departments

  • Business and bulk orders: business@thistleandpine.co.uk – for orders of 20 or more units of the same item, orders over £1,000, VAT invoices and payment terms.
  • Privacy and data requests: privacy@thistleandpine.co.uk – contact our Data Protection Officer about your personal data.

Before you contact us

You might find your answer faster in our Help Centre:

  • Track your order using the link in your dispatch email.
  • Start a return at thistleandpine.co.uk/returns.
  • Cancel or change the address on an undispatched order in My account.

Stay safe

We will never ask for your card details by phone, email or live chat. If you're unsure whether a message is really from us, contact us using the details on this page.
