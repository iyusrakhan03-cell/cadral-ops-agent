E-Gift Cards

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Not sure what to choose? A Thistle & Pine e-gift card lets them pick their own favourite cookware, bedding or candles. Our gift cards are sent by email, so they're perfect for last-minute presents.

How our e-gift cards work

  • Choose any value from £10 to £250.
  • Your e-gift card is emailed with a unique code that can be used at checkout on our website.
  • Each e-gift card is valid for 24 months from the date of purchase.
  • Gift cards can be used across multiple orders until the balance runs out.

Do you sell physical gift cards?

No. We only sell e-gift cards. We don't sell plastic or paper gift cards, and our gift cards can't be posted.

How to buy an e-gift card

  1. Go to the Gift Cards section of our shop.
  2. Choose an amount between £10 and £250.
  3. Enter the recipient's name and email address, and add a personal message if you like.
  4. Pay at checkout using any of our accepted payment methods.

There's no delivery charge for e-gift cards. Please note that e-gift cards are excluded from our Black Friday promotion and from most discount codes.

How to spend an e-gift card

At checkout, enter the gift card code in the box marked Gift card and select Apply. The amount will be taken off your order total. If your order is more than the remaining balance, you can pay the difference by card, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay or Klarna.

Gift cards can only be used on our website, as we don't have any shops.

Lost your code?

If the gift card email has gone missing, we can resend the code to the purchaser's email address. Ask the person who bought the card to contact us with their order number, and we'll resend it. For security reasons, we can only send the code to the email address used to buy it.

Returns and refunds

Gift cards are non-returnable and can't be exchanged for cash. If you return an item that you paid for with a gift card, your refund will be issued as a new e-gift card.

Expiry

Your e-gift card expires 24 months after it was purchased. Any remaining balance after that date can't be used, so we recommend spending it in good time. The expiry date is shown in the gift card email.

Business gifting

Looking to buy gift cards for staff or clients in bulk? Contact business@thistleandpine.co.uk and our team will help.
