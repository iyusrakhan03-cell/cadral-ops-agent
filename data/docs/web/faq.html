Frequently Asked Questions

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Here are the questions our Customer Care team hears most often. If you can't find what you need, our team is on hand by email, phone or live chat.

Ordering

Do you have a shop I can visit?

No. Thistle & Pine is an online-only retailer, so we don't have any physical shops and we don't offer click & collect. Every order is sent from our warehouse in Leeds.

Can I add something to an order I've already placed?

Unfortunately not. Once an order is placed we can't add items to it, so please place a second order for anything else you need.

Can I change my delivery address or cancel my order?

Yes, free of charge, as long as your order hasn't been dispatched yet. You can do this in your account or by contacting us. Once an order has been dispatched it can't be cancelled, but you can refuse the delivery or return it.

Delivery

How much is UK delivery?

Standard delivery (Royal Mail Tracked 48) costs £3.95 and takes 3–5 working days. It's free on orders of £50 or more. Express delivery with DPD costs £6.95 for next working day delivery when you order before 2pm Monday to Thursday.

When will my order be dispatched?

Orders placed before 1pm on a working day are dispatched the same day. We'll email you a tracking link as soon as your parcel leaves us.

Do you deliver at weekends?

No, we don't deliver on Saturdays or Sundays.

Do you deliver to BFPO addresses?

Sorry, we're unable to deliver to BFPO addresses.

Do you ship outside the UK?

We deliver to the Republic of Ireland, France, Germany, the Netherlands and Belgium for a flat £14.95 (free on orders over £150). See our International Delivery page for details.

Returns and refunds

How long do I have to return something?

You have 30 days from delivery to return most items, unused and in their original packaging. UK returns are free using a prepaid Royal Mail label from our Returns Portal at thistleandpine.co.uk/returns.

Can I exchange an item for a different colour or size?

We don't offer direct exchanges. Please return the item for a refund and place a new order for the one you'd like.

How long will my refund take?

We process refunds within 5 working days of your return arriving at our warehouse. Your bank may then take a further 3–5 working days to show the money in your account.

What can't be returned?

Personalised or engraved items, opened pillows and duvets (for hygiene reasons), gift cards and items marked "Final Sale" can't be returned unless they're faulty.

Payment and discounts

Can I use more than one discount code?

Only one discount code can be used per order, and codes can't be added after an order has been placed.

Do you price match?

No, we don't price match with other retailers.

Will you ever ask for my card details over the phone?

Never. We will never ask for your card details by phone, email or live chat. If someone does, please don't share them and let us know.

Products

Can I put my cast iron pan in the dishwasher?

No. Pine Pro cast iron is hand wash only. Dry it straight away and rub it with a thin layer of oil. Dishwasher damage to cast iron isn't covered by the Pine Pro guarantee.

Do you offer gift wrapping?

Yes. Gift wrapping is £3.50 per item and includes a personal gift message. It's available all year round, not just at Christmas.

Do you offer discounts for large or business orders?

Yes. For 20 or more units of the same item, or orders over £1,000, please email business@thistleandpine.co.uk to ask about our tiered business discount.
