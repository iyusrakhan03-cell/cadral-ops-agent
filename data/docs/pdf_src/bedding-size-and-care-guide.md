# Bedding Size and Care Guide

Version 1.3 — effective 1 May 2026

Choosing the right size and looking after your bedding properly means a better night's sleep and bedding that lasts for years. This guide covers UK bed and duvet sizes, how to measure, and how to wash and care for our linen and cotton bedding, pillows and duvets.

## 1. UK bed sizes

All our fitted sheets and mattress-related bedding are made to standard UK mattress sizes.

| Bed size | Mattress size (cm) |
|---|---|
| Single | 90 × 190 |
| Small double | 120 × 190 |
| Double | 135 × 190 |
| King | 150 × 200 |
| Super king | 180 × 200 |

Please note that UK sizes differ from European and US sizes. A European "double" or a US "queen" is not the same as a UK double or king, so always check the measurements in centimetres.

## 2. Duvet and duvet cover sizes

| Duvet size | Dimensions (cm) |
|---|---|
| Single | 135 × 200 |
| Double | 200 × 200 |
| King | 230 × 220 |
| Super king | 260 × 220 |

We don't make a separate small double duvet. For a small double bed, most customers choose a double duvet and duvet cover.

## 3. How to measure your bed

- Measure the width and length of your mattress, not the bed frame.
- Measure the depth of your mattress, including any mattress topper. Our fitted sheets have deep elasticated corners to fit most mattresses and toppers.
- If you like a generous overhang, you may prefer a duvet one size larger than your bed, for example a king duvet on a double bed.

## 4. Caring for linen bedding

Our linen bedding is made from European flax and is OEKO-TEX certified.

- Wash at 40°C on a gentle cycle with a mild detergent.
- Tumble dry on a low heat, or line dry.
- Linen softens with each wash, so it will feel even better over time.
- Linen has a naturally relaxed, crumpled look and doesn't need ironing. If you prefer a smoother finish, iron on a medium heat while slightly damp.
- Avoid bleach and optical brighteners, which can weaken the fibres.
- Wash dark colours separately for the first few washes.

## 5. Caring for cotton bedding

- Follow the instructions on the care label.
- Turn duvet covers and pillowcases inside out and fasten the buttons before washing to protect them.
- Remove promptly from the machine to reduce creasing.

## 6. Pillows and duvets

- Use a pillow protector and a mattress protector to keep pillows and duvets fresh.
- Plump pillows daily and air your duvet regularly.
- Check the care label before washing pillows or duvets. Larger duvets may need a launderette-sized machine.
- Make sure pillows and duvets are completely dry before using them again.

### Hygiene and returns

For hygiene reasons, pillows and duvets cannot be returned once they have been opened (removed from their sealed packaging), unless they are faulty. Please check the size carefully before opening. Unopened pillows and duvets can be returned within 30 days of delivery.

## 7. Storing bedding

Store clean, fully dry bedding in a cool, dry, well-ventilated place. Breathable cotton storage bags are better than plastic, which can trap moisture.

## 8. Warranty

All our bedding is covered by our standard 12-month warranty against manufacturing defects, such as split seams or faulty fastenings. Damage caused by washing at the wrong temperature or using bleach isn't covered.
