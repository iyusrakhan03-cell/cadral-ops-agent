# Warranty and Guarantees

Version 2.1 — effective 1 February 2026

We choose our products to last, and we stand behind them. This document explains the warranties and guarantees that come with products bought from Thistle & Pine Ltd, what they cover, and how to make a claim. Our warranties are in addition to your statutory rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015, which are not affected.

## 1. Warranty periods

| Product type | Warranty period |
|---|---|
| All products (standard warranty) | 12 months from delivery |
| Furniture | 2 years from delivery |
| Electrical items (kettles, toasters, lamps) | 2 years from delivery |
| Pine Pro cookware range | 10-year guarantee against manufacturing defects |

All warranty periods start from the date the item was delivered to you. The warranty period for each product is also shown on its product page.

## 2. What our warranty covers

Our warranty covers faults caused by defects in materials or manufacture that appear during normal domestic use in line with the care instructions. Examples include:

- a handle that works loose on a pan
- a seam on a duvet cover that comes apart
- a drawer runner that fails on a bedside table
- a kettle that stops heating
- a lamp switch that stops working

## 3. What our warranty does not cover

Our warranties do not cover:

- normal wear and tear, such as fading or light scratching over time
- accidental damage, such as dropping a dish or a pan
- damage caused by misuse or not following the care instructions
- commercial or business use, unless agreed with us in writing
- items that have been altered or repaired by someone other than us
- natural variation in materials such as wood grain, linen texture or glaze colour

## 4. The Pine Pro 10-year guarantee

Every item in our Pine Pro cookware range comes with a 10-year guarantee against manufacturing defects. This includes the Pine Pro cast iron and Pine Pro stainless steel collections.

The Pine Pro guarantee does not cover:

- misuse, for example using a pan for anything other than cooking
- damage from overheating, such as heating an empty pan on high for a long time, which can cause warping or discolouration
- dishwasher damage to cast iron. Pine Pro cast iron is hand wash only and is not dishwasher safe, so rust or loss of seasoning caused by the dishwasher is not covered
- damage from exceeding the maximum oven temperature: 260°C for Pine Pro cast iron and 220°C for Pine Pro stainless steel

Pine Pro stainless steel pans are dishwasher safe, so using a dishwasher does not affect the guarantee on stainless steel.

Please read our Cookware Care Guide for full instructions.

## 5. Your statutory rights

If an item is faulty, the Consumer Rights Act 2015 gives you the following rights, regardless of our warranty:

- within 30 days of delivery: a full refund
- from 30 days up to 6 months: a repair or replacement, or a refund if a repair or replacement is not possible
- after 6 months: claims are handled under our warranty

## 6. How to make a warranty claim

To make a claim, please email support@thistleandpine.co.uk with:

- your order number
- a short description of the problem
- clear photos showing the fault, and the whole product

You can also start a claim on live chat and upload photos there.

Our team will review your claim and normally reply within a few working days. If we need to inspect the item, we will send you a prepaid returns label, or arrange a free collection for 2-person delivery items.

## 7. How we put it right

If your claim is accepted, we will repair, replace or refund the item. We decide which option is most appropriate based on the product and the fault. Where an identical replacement is no longer available, we will offer a similar product or a refund.

A replacement item is covered for the remainder of the original warranty period.

## 8. Products bought as gifts

Warranty claims for gifts can be made by the recipient, but we will need the original order number. If you don't have it, ask the person who bought the gift to contact us.
