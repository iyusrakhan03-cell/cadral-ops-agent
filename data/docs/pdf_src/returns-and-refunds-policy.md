# Returns and Refunds Policy

Version 3.2 — effective 1 November 2026

This policy explains how to return items bought from Thistle & Pine Ltd ("Thistle & Pine", "we", "us") and how and when we refund you. It applies to all orders placed on our website. It does not affect your statutory rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 or the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013.

## 1. Our 30-day returns promise

You can return most items within 30 days of delivery for a refund. To be accepted, items must be:

- unused, and in the condition in which you received them
- in their original packaging, including any tags, inserts and protective wrapping
- returned with the returns reference generated by our Returns Portal

The 30 days are counted from the date the item was delivered to you, not the date of your order.

## 2. Christmas extended returns

Orders placed between 1 November 2026 and 24 December 2026 can be returned until 31 January 2027. All other conditions in this policy still apply, including the list of non-returnable items.

## 3. How to return an item (UK)

UK returns are free. To start a return:

- Go to the Returns Portal at thistleandpine.co.uk/returns.
- Enter your order number and the email address used to place the order.
- Select the items you are returning and a reason.
- Print the prepaid Royal Mail label, or show the QR code at a Post Office.
- Pack the items securely, attach the label and drop the parcel at a Post Office or Royal Mail drop-off point.

Keep your proof of postage until your refund has been processed.

We are an online-only business and do not accept returns in person at our warehouse.

## 4. Large 2-person delivery items

Furniture and storage items over 20kg that were delivered by our 2-person service can't be posted back. Instead, start your return through the Returns Portal and we will arrange a collection from your home.

A collection fee of £25 is deducted from your refund. The collection fee is not charged if the item is faulty or arrived damaged.

## 5. International returns

Customers in the Republic of Ireland, France, Germany, the Netherlands and Belgium can return items within the same 30-day period. International customers pay their own return postage, unless the item is faulty or damaged, in which case we will cover the cost. We recommend using a tracked service.

## 6. Items that cannot be returned

For hygiene, safety or practical reasons, the following items cannot be returned unless they are faulty:

- personalised or engraved items
- pillows and duvets that have been opened (removed from their sealed packaging)
- gift cards
- items marked "Final Sale" on the product page

Unopened pillows and duvets in their original sealed packaging can be returned as normal.

## 7. Exchanges

We do not offer direct exchanges. If you would like a different size, colour or product, please return the original item for a refund and place a new order.

## 8. Refunds

### 8.1 Timing

We process your refund within 5 working days of your return arriving at our warehouse. We will email you when your refund has been issued. Your bank or card provider may then take a further 3–5 working days to show the money in your account.

### 8.2 Payment method

Refunds are made to your original payment method:

| How you paid | How you are refunded |
|---|---|
| Card, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay | Back to the same card or account |
| Klarna Pay in 3 | Refunded to Klarna, which updates your payment plan |
| Thistle & Pine gift card | Issued as a new e-gift card |

We cannot refund to a different card or bank account.

### 8.3 Delivery charges

The original delivery charge is refunded only if:

- you return the whole order within 14 days of delivery, using your statutory cancellation right; or
- the item is faulty or arrived damaged.

In all other cases, the delivery charge is not refundable. Gift wrapping charges are refunded together with the item they relate to.

### 8.4 Pine Points

If your return is refunded, any Pine Points earned on the returned items are deducted from your balance.

### 8.5 Discounted items

If you used a discount code or bought during a promotion, you will be refunded the amount you actually paid for the item, not its full price.

## 9. Faulty and damaged items

If an item arrives damaged, please report it within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the item and its packaging. Faulty items are covered by the Consumer Rights Act 2015: you are entitled to a full refund within 30 days, and to a repair or replacement up to 6 months, after which our warranty applies.

We pay the return postage for faulty or damaged items. For low-value items under £20, we may refund you without needing the item back.

Please see our Warranty and Guarantees document for claims after 6 months.

## 10. Returns we cannot accept

If an item is returned used, damaged by the customer, incomplete or outside the returns period, we may be unable to refund it. In this case we will contact you and, where possible, send the item back to you.
