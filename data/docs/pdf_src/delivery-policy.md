# UK Delivery Policy

Version 2.4 — effective 1 March 2026

This policy sets out how Thistle & Pine Ltd delivers orders within the United Kingdom, how much delivery costs and what to do if something goes wrong. For deliveries to the Republic of Ireland, France, Germany, the Netherlands and Belgium, please see our International Delivery page.

## 1. Delivery options at a glance

| Service | Cost | Delivery time |
|---|---|---|
| Standard (Royal Mail Tracked 48) | £3.95, free on orders of £50 or more | 3–5 working days |
| Express (DPD next working day) | £6.95 | Next working day if ordered before 2pm Mon–Thu |
| Highlands & Islands, Isle of Man, Channel Islands, Northern Ireland | £8.95 | 5–7 working days, Standard only |
| 2-person delivery (large items) | £29 | 7–14 working days |

All delivery prices include VAT. Working days are Monday to Friday, excluding UK bank holidays.

## 2. Standard delivery

Standard delivery is sent with Royal Mail Tracked 48 and normally arrives within 3–5 working days. It costs £3.95, and it is free when your order totals £50 or more (after any discount has been applied).

## 3. Express delivery

Express delivery is sent with DPD for next working day delivery and costs £6.95.

- Order before 2pm Monday to Thursday for delivery the next working day.
- Orders placed after 2pm on Thursday, or at any time on Friday, Saturday or Sunday, will arrive on Tuesday.

DPD will send you a one-hour delivery window by text or email on the morning of delivery.

Express delivery is not available to the Scottish Highlands and Islands, the Isle of Man, the Channel Islands or Northern Ireland, or for 2-person delivery items.

## 4. Highlands & Islands, Isle of Man, Channel Islands and Northern Ireland

Deliveries to these areas are sent by Standard delivery only, take 5–7 working days and cost £8.95. The free-delivery threshold of £50 does not apply to these areas, so the £8.95 charge applies to every order regardless of its value.

## 5. 2-person delivery for large items

Furniture and storage items weighing over 20kg are marked "2-person delivery" on the product page. These are delivered by a two-person DPD team, who will carry the item into your home.

- The charge is £29 per order.
- Delivery takes 7–14 working days.
- DPD will text you to book a delivery slot that suits you.
- 2-person items can only be delivered to UK mainland addresses and cannot be shipped internationally.

If your order contains both 2-person items and smaller items, the smaller items may be sent separately by Standard delivery.

## 6. Dispatch and tracking

Orders placed before 1pm on a working day are dispatched the same day. Orders placed after 1pm, or at the weekend or on a bank holiday, are dispatched on the next working day.

When your order is dispatched, we will email you a tracking link. You can use it to follow your parcel and, with DPD, to change your delivery preferences.

## 7. Days we do not deliver

We do not deliver on Saturdays or Sundays. We are also unable to deliver to BFPO addresses.

## 8. If you are not in

Royal Mail will either leave your parcel in a safe place, deliver it to a neighbour, or leave a card so you can collect it from your local delivery office. DPD will offer you options to redirect or rearrange your delivery. Parcels that cannot be delivered are returned to us, and we will contact you.

## 9. Late or lost parcels

If your Standard delivery tracking has not updated for 5 working days, please contact us. We will open an investigation with the courier, which can take up to 10 working days. Once the investigation is complete, we will either refund you or send a replacement.

Please don't place a second order while an investigation is open, as you may be charged twice.

## 10. Damaged deliveries

If your order arrives damaged, please report it within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the item and the packaging, by email to support@thistleandpine.co.uk or via live chat. We will put it right as quickly as possible.
