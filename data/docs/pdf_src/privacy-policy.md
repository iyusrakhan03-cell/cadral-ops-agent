# Privacy Policy

Version 3.0 — effective 1 April 2026

This privacy policy explains how Thistle & Pine Ltd collects, uses and protects your personal data when you visit our website, place an order or contact us. We take your privacy seriously and process your data in line with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

## 1. Who we are

Thistle & Pine Ltd is the data controller for your personal data. We are registered in England & Wales under company number 11873204, and our address is Unit 4, Kirkstall Trade Park, Leeds, LS5 3BT.

Our Data Protection Officer (DPO) can be contacted at privacy@thistleandpine.co.uk.

## 2. The data we collect

We collect the following types of personal data:

- Identity and contact data: your name, email address, delivery and billing addresses and phone number.
- Order data: the products you buy, order values, delivery choices, returns and refunds.
- Account data: your login details, saved addresses, Pine Points balance and, if you choose to give it, your date of birth for our birthday bonus.
- Payment data: we receive confirmation of payment from our payment providers. We do not store your full card number.
- Communication data: messages you send us by email, phone or live chat, including any photos you upload.
- Marketing preferences: whether you have signed up to our newsletter.
- Technical data: your IP address, browser type and how you use our website, collected through cookies.

## 3. How we use your data

| Purpose | Lawful basis |
|---|---|
| Processing and delivering your order | Performance of a contract |
| Handling returns, refunds and warranty claims | Performance of a contract |
| Running your account and Pine Points | Performance of a contract |
| Answering your questions and complaints | Legitimate interests |
| Keeping records for tax and accounting | Legal obligation |
| Sending our newsletter and offers | Consent |
| Preventing fraud and keeping our website secure | Legitimate interests |
| Verifying student, NHS or Blue Light discounts | Consent |

## 4. Who we share your data with

We share your data only where needed to run our business, with:

- delivery companies, such as Royal Mail and DPD, so they can deliver your order
- payment providers, including PayPal and Klarna, to take payments and issue refunds
- our email, website hosting and customer service software providers
- our accountants and professional advisers
- the police or other authorities, if we are required to by law

All our suppliers must keep your data secure and use it only on our instructions.

We never sell your personal data to anyone.

## 5. International transfers

Some of our service providers may process data outside the UK. Where this happens, we make sure appropriate safeguards are in place, such as an adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses.

## 6. How long we keep your data

- Order data is kept for 6 years, as required by UK tax law.
- Marketing data is kept until you unsubscribe.
- Account data is kept while your account is active. If you ask us to close your account, we will delete your account data, except order records we must keep for tax purposes.
- Customer service records are kept for as long as needed to deal with your query and any related claim.

## 7. Your rights

Under data protection law, you have the right to:

- access the personal data we hold about you
- have inaccurate data corrected
- have your data deleted, where we have no legal reason to keep it
- object to or restrict certain processing
- withdraw consent for marketing at any time
- data portability, in some circumstances

To make a request, email privacy@thistleandpine.co.uk. We will respond within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm your identity before we act on your request. There is normally no charge.

## 8. Marketing

We will only send you marketing emails if you have opted in. Every email includes an unsubscribe link, and you can also change your preferences in your account. Service emails about your orders, such as dispatch confirmations, will still be sent after you unsubscribe from marketing.

## 9. Cookies

We use essential cookies to make our website work, such as remembering your basket. With your permission, we also use analytics and marketing cookies. You can change your cookie choices at any time using the cookie settings link at the bottom of every page.

## 10. Security

Our website uses encryption to protect your data. Access to customer data is restricted to staff who need it to do their jobs. We will never ask for your card details by phone, email or live chat.

## 11. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, please contact our DPO first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK data protection regulator, at ico.org.uk.

## 12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on our website.
