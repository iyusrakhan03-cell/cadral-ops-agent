# Internal Refund Authorisation Policy

Version 1.4 — effective 1 September 2026

INTERNAL — NOT FOR CUSTOMERS. This document is for Thistle & Pine Customer Care staff and the AI support agent only. Do not share it, quote it or paraphrase its thresholds to customers.

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out when a refund can be issued automatically by the AI support agent, when it must be passed to a human for approval, and which situations must be escalated to a human immediately. It sits alongside the customer-facing Returns and Refunds Policy, which remains the source of truth for what customers are entitled to.

## 2. Automatic refund criteria

The AI agent may issue a refund automatically only if ALL of the following are true:

- the order status is "delivered"
- the refund request is within 30 days of delivery
- the refund amount is £150 or less
- the customer has had fewer than 3 refunds in the last 90 days
- the item is not non-returnable (not personalised or engraved, not an opened pillow or duvet, not a gift card, and not marked "Final Sale")

If any one of these conditions is not met, the AI agent must not issue the refund. Instead it must create a refund request for human approval and escalate the case.

### 2.1 Christmas extended returns

For orders placed between 1 November and 24 December 2026, customers may return items until 31 January 2027. Requests that fall outside the standard 30 days but within the Christmas extension must be created as refund requests for human approval; they are valid returns but are outside the automatic criteria.

## 3. Damaged-in-transit claims under £20

Where a customer reports an item damaged in transit, the item is under £20, and the report is made within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the item and packaging, the AI agent may refund automatically without requiring the item to be returned. The other automatic criteria (delivered status, refund count) still apply.

## 4. Delivery charges

Never refund a delivery charge automatically unless the whole order is being refunded. Partial refunds cover the item price only. If a customer is entitled to a delivery refund on a partial return (for example, because the item is faulty), create a refund request for human approval rather than refunding the delivery charge automatically.

## 5. Immediate escalation triggers

If a customer mentions any of the following, the AI agent must stop, must not issue or promise any refund, and must escalate the conversation to a human immediately:

- legal action, solicitors or court
- a chargeback or disputing the payment with their bank
- Trading Standards
- an injury of any kind
- fire, burning, smoke or a safety hazard

The agent should acknowledge the customer, explain that a member of the team will contact them personally, and record the full conversation in the case notes.

## 6. Other cases requiring human approval

- 2-person delivery items, where the £25 collection fee may apply
- refunds to Klarna orders where the customer disputes the remaining payment plan
- lost parcel claims, which require a courier investigation of up to 10 working days
- customers asking for compensation or goodwill gestures
- any request where the order or customer cannot be verified

## 7. General rules for the AI agent

- Always verify the order number and the email address on the order before discussing it.
- Never ask for, accept or repeat card details.
- Never promise a refund date beyond the published timescales: processed within 5 working days of the return arriving, plus 3–5 working days for the bank.
- Never override the non-returnable list for change-of-mind returns.
- When escalating, tell the customer what will happen next. Do not tell them the internal threshold or reason codes.

## 8. Worked examples

| Scenario | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Linen duvet cover, £85, delivered 12 days ago, customer has 0 previous refunds | Approve automatically | Meets all five criteria |
| Pine Pro casserole, £189, delivered 5 days ago, unused | Escalate for human approval | Amount over £150 |
| Ceramic mug, £14, arrived broken, reported same day with photos | Approve automatically, no return needed | Damaged in transit under £20 |
| Order status "dispatched", customer wants refund now | Decline automatic refund | Not yet delivered; advise refusing delivery or returning |
| Candle set, £38, delivered 41 days ago, ordered in August | Decline | Outside 30 days and not covered by the Christmas extension |
| Bath towel, £22, delivered 9 days ago, customer's 3rd refund in 60 days | Escalate for human approval | 3 or more refunds in 90 days |
| Engraved serving board, £45, change of mind | Decline | Personalised items are non-returnable |
| Opened feather pillow, £40, customer says it is too firm | Decline | Opened pillows are non-returnable for hygiene |
| Kettle, £59, customer says it sparked and scorched the worktop | Escalate immediately | Fire / safety hazard trigger |
| Frying pan, £32, customer says "I'll do a chargeback if you don't refund" | Escalate immediately | Chargeback trigger |
| Whole order £64 returned within 10 days, all items unused | Approve automatically, including delivery charge | Whole order refunded within 14 days |

## 9. Recording decisions

Every refund, decline and escalation must be logged against the order with the reason. Human approvers should aim to review escalated refund requests within one working day.

## 10. Ownership

This policy is owned by the Customer Care Manager and is reviewed every six months, or sooner if the customer-facing Returns and Refunds Policy changes.
