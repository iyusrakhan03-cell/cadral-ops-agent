## Delivery (UK)
- Standard (Royal Mail Tracked 48): 3–5 working days, £3.95, FREE on orders of £50 or more
- Express (DPD next working day): £6.95, order before 2pm Mon–Thu for next working day. Orders after 2pm Thursday, or on Fri/Sat/Sun, arrive Tuesday.
- Highlands & Islands, Isle of Man, Channel Islands, Northern Ireland: Standard only, 5–7 working days, £8.95 (free-delivery threshold does NOT apply)
- Large items (furniture/storage over 20kg, marked "2-person delivery"): DPD 2-person delivery, £29, 7–14 working days, delivery slot booked by text
- No Saturday or Sunday deliveries. No BFPO.
- Orders placed before 1pm on a working day are dispatched the same day.
- Tracking link emailed when the order is dispatched.
- Lost parcel: if tracking has not updated for 5 working days (Standard) contact us; we open a courier investigation which takes up to 10 working days, then we refund or resend.

## International delivery
- Only to: Republic of Ireland, France, Germany, Netherlands, Belgium
- £14.95 flat, 7–10 working days, free on orders over £150
- EU orders: import VAT and duties are collected at checkout (DDP), no extra charges on delivery
- Large 2-person items cannot be shipped internationally
- International returns: customer pays return postage unless the item is faulty

## Returns
- 30 days from delivery to return most items, unused, in original packaging
- UK returns are FREE using the prepaid Royal Mail label from the Returns Portal (thistleandpine.co.uk/returns)
- Large 2-person items: we collect, a £25 collection fee is deducted from the refund (free if faulty)
- Non-returnable: personalised/engraved items, opened pillows & duvets (hygiene), gift cards, items marked "Final Sale"
- Christmas extended returns: orders placed 1 November – 24 December 2026 can be returned until 31 January 2027
- Exchanges: we do not do direct exchanges; return for a refund and place a new order

## Refunds
- Processed within 5 working days of the return arriving at our warehouse
- Refunded to the original payment method; banks can take a further 3–5 working days
- Original delivery charge is refunded only if the whole order is returned within 14 days (cancellation right) or the item is faulty
- Klarna orders: refund goes to Klarna, which updates the payment plan
- Gift card payments: refunded as a new gift card

## Damaged / faulty items
- Damaged in transit: report within 48 hours of delivery with photos (item + packaging) via support email or chat
- Faulty items: covered by the Consumer Rights Act 2015 — full refund within 30 days; repair/replacement after that up to 6 months, then per warranty
- Faulty or damaged items: we pay return postage, or for low-value items (under £20) we may refund without needing the item back

## Warranty / guarantees
- Standard warranty: 12 months from delivery on all products
- "Pine Pro" cookware range: 10-year guarantee against manufacturing defects (not misuse, overheating or dishwasher damage on cast iron)
- Furniture: 2-year warranty
- Electrical items (kettles, toasters, lamps): 2-year warranty
- Warranty claims: email order number + photos; we repair, replace or refund

## Order changes & cancellation
- Cancel or change address free of charge any time BEFORE the order is dispatched (via account or support)
- After dispatch an order cannot be cancelled — refuse delivery or return it instead
- 14-day cancellation right (Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013) for online purchases
- Items cannot be added to an order already placed; place a second order

## Product care (key facts)
- Cast iron (Pine Pro cast iron): hand wash only, dry immediately, rub with thin layer of oil; NOT dishwasher safe; oven safe to 260°C
- Stainless steel Pine Pro pans: dishwasher safe, oven safe to 220°C, induction compatible
- Non-stick pans: dishwasher safe but hand wash extends life; avoid metal utensils; oven safe to 180°C
- Wooden chopping boards: hand wash only, never soak, oil monthly with food-safe mineral oil
- Stoneware tableware: dishwasher & microwave safe; not oven-to-hob
- Linen bedding: wash at 40°C, tumble dry low, softens with each wash
- Candles: trim wick to 5mm, first burn 2–3 hours until full melt pool, never burn more than 4 hours, burn time ~45 hours (large), ~25 hours (small)
